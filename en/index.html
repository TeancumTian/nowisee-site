Energy & Power1712

Steam engine蒸汽机

The first practical heat engine to turn the heat from burning coal into mechanical work, helping launch the Industrial Revolution.

Why it works 原理

When water turns into steam, its volume expands by about 1700 times. When the steam cools, it condenses back into water, leaving a vacuum—this expansion and contraction push the piston, converting the heat from burning coal into mechanical work (conservation of energy, phase change).

水变成蒸汽时体积膨胀约 1700 倍，蒸汽遇冷又会凝结成水、留下真空——让这一胀一缩去推活塞，烧煤的热就变成了机械功（能量守恒、相变）。

Questions常见问题

What do I need?

An iPhone or iPad running iOS / iPadOS 26 or later. The interface and text can be English or Chinese, or both side by side.

Do I need to be online or sign up?

No. Everything is inside the app and works offline, with no ads and no data collection. Your learning record stays on your device, and you can export a backup.

Can I trust the numbers?

Every domain comes with a sources and verification record listing where its key numbers and dates come from. If you find a mistake, please tell us.

I got a new device. Do I keep the Full Version?

Yes. Sign in with the same Apple Account and tap Settings → Full Version → Restore Purchases. Members of your Family Sharing group can use it too.
